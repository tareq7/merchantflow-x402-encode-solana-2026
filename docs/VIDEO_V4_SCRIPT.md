## Spoken performance script

[conversational, relaxed] I run an online store selling TV screen protectors. And every day, I have to make the same call: where should I spend my advertising budget?

Sometimes Amazon tells me a campaign is doing great. Seven times return on ad spend. Sounds good, right?

[thoughtful] But there's a catch. That product might have ten days of stock left, while the next shipment takes thirty days to arrive. If I increase the budget now, I could be paying to sell something I won't have.

That's why I built SellerPilot.

[clear, matter-of-fact] It puts advertising results and inventory together, so I can see the problem before it's too late.

Here's a synthetic example. Rather than burying me in charts, it points to the campaign that needs attention first.

I open the recommendation and check the evidence: how much stock is left, how the ad is performing, and how long restocking takes. If the data doesn't add up, I want to know that too.

[curious] What if I spend fifteen percent more?

I can test it here. The inventory runway changes, and the assumptions are shown. It's a scenario to think through, not a promise about sales.

[confident, natural] Then I make the decision. The app doesn't quietly change my campaigns. I review the action, stage it, and export it when I'm ready.

I can import a real Amazon Sponsored Products report, along with my own product costs. For Noon, I added a guided column mapper, because exports don't always arrive in the same format.

When costs or inventory figures are missing, SellerPilot flags them before I approve anything.

And the whole interface works in English and Arabic, including right-to-left.

[thoughtful, personal] Meta Muse Spark helps explain these verified decisions in normal language. We tested Contributor using synthetic inventory data. The app's live model connection stays private while we finish checking it.

[calm, sincere] I built this because I wanted advertising and inventory to make sense together. So before I spend another riyal, I can see the risk and decide for myself.
